# Olingo Product Vision

## North Star

Olingo helps an A2-to-B1 adult learner become quicker and more confident at constructing accurate spoken Italian sentences for real one-to-one conversation.

The app is not primarily a vocabulary recogniser. It is a daily sentence-production game: the learner retrieves useful chunks, transforms them quickly, repairs mistakes, and uses the language in realistic social situations.

## Primary Users

- Main learner: A2 moving toward B1 Italian, wants faster conversation and richer culture/news chat.
- Partner learner: another adult learner in the same household, also studying Italian.
- Future optional use: trusted friends or family can join collaborative games, but v1 should keep account and progress models clean enough for multi-user use.

Do not hard-code real personal names into public seed data. Use configurable persona slots such as "Milan family contact", "Italian friend", "partner", and "culture conversation partner".

## Target Conversations

The core scenarios should support:

- One-to-one social chat.
- Culture, books, films, music, food, travel, and current affairs.
- Light news discussion in British English prompts and Italian responses.
- Conversations with family/friends from Milan.
- Practice with a spouse/partner learning at the same time.
- Everyday travel/restaurant situations only where they support conversation confidence.

## Product Promise

Fifteen minutes a day should leave the learner feeling:

- "I produced Italian today, not just recognised it."
- "The app remembered what I got wrong and turned it into useful practice."
- "I can see my fluency improving."
- "I am moving through a story/world, not grinding through flashcards."

## V1 Shape

V1 should ship as a mobile-first and desktop-friendly web app with:

- Daily Sprint: one 15-minute guided session chosen by the app.
- Sentence Forge: fast typed sentence construction from realistic prompts.
- Mistake Gym: spaced practice generated from the learner's own errors.
- Scene Quests: photoreal locations with button-driven interaction.
- Fluency Stats: measurable progress in speed, accuracy, repair rate, and phrase mastery.
- Narrative Progress: locations, people, and conversation arcs unlock as ability grows.

## Experience Principles

- Bright, gamified, adult, and focused. Fun without feeling childish.
- Photoreal scenes as the primary game wrapper, especially for village/city locations.
- Button interactivity should drive choices, actions, tone, and next steps.
- Every activity should require production, transformation, or repair.
- Feedback should be kind, quick, and actionable.
- Do not overwhelm the learner with grammar lectures during the flow. Save explanations for drill summaries and optional detail panels.

## Game World

The game world should feel like an Italian neighbourhood rather than a flat lesson list. The learner moves through photoreal places and social moments:

- A Milan cafe.
- A family kitchen.
- A small bookshop.
- A railway platform.
- A cultural event or cinema foyer.
- A piazza/newsstand.

Each place has people, tasks, and recurring conversation threads. The app chooses today's quest based on due practice, learner goals, and recent mistakes.

## Motivation Model

Prioritise:

- Fluency stats.
- Narrative progress.
- Mastered phrase collections.
- Daily streaks as a secondary habit aid.
- XP only as a light pacing device.

Avoid shallow tapping rewards. Points should mostly come from producing useful Italian and repairing mistakes.

## V1 Success Criteria

After four weeks of daily use, the learner should be able to show:

- Increased typed sentence speed for familiar frames.
- Fewer repeat errors in tracked mistake categories.
- More mastered sentence chunks and discourse phrases.
- Higher confidence in short one-to-one conversations about personal life, plans, culture, and news.
- A visible trail of completed scenes and narrative progress.

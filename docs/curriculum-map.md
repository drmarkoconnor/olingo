# Curriculum Map

## Level Target

The first major curriculum band is A2-to-B1.

The learner should move from short rehearsed sentences toward connected, spontaneous-ish responses about familiar topics, opinions, culture, people, and current events.

## Skill Progression

### Band 1: Reliable Everyday Sentences

Goal: produce accurate core sentences without freezing.

Focus:

- Present tense of high-frequency verbs.
- Articles and noun gender.
- Adjective agreement.
- Questions and answers.
- Likes, wants, needs, plans.
- Time, frequency, and basic connectors.

Example tasks:

- Introduce yourself and ask a follow-up.
- Explain what you usually do.
- Order and clarify politely.
- Describe a person, place, or film.

### Band 2: Social Conversation Frames

Goal: keep a one-to-one chat moving.

Focus:

- Follow-up questions.
- Opinions.
- Agreement and disagreement.
- Softening.
- Conversation repair.
- Past events with passato prossimo and imperfetto.

Example tasks:

- Ask someone about their week.
- Discuss a film or meal.
- Explain what happened yesterday.
- Say you did not understand and ask for repetition.

### Band 3: Culture And News Chat

Goal: talk about a simple article, headline, or video.

Focus:

- Reported gist.
- "I read that..."
- Cause and consequence.
- Comparisons.
- Tentative opinions.
- More complex connectors.

Example tasks:

- Summarise a short headline in one sentence.
- Ask what someone thinks about a news item.
- Compare life in Italy and the UK.
- Explain why an event is interesting or surprising.

### Band 4: Narrative Confidence

Goal: tell short stories and connect ideas.

Focus:

- Past tense contrast.
- Sequencing.
- Emotional reactions.
- Clarifying details.
- Hypothetical or conditional phrases.

Example tasks:

- Tell a short story about a journey.
- Explain a misunderstanding.
- Describe a family event.
- Say what you would do in a situation.

## Scene Map

### Milan Cafe

Purpose: social openings, opinions, food/drink, follow-up questions.

Scene actions:

- Greet.
- Order.
- Ask opinion.
- React.
- Clarify.
- Continue conversation.

### Family Kitchen

Purpose: one-to-one family conversation and household chat.

Scene actions:

- Offer help.
- Explain plans.
- Ask about someone's day.
- Tell a small story.
- Compare preferences.

### Bookshop

Purpose: culture, recommendations, opinions.

Scene actions:

- Ask for a recommendation.
- Describe taste.
- Compare books/films.
- Say why something interests you.

### Newsstand Or Piazza

Purpose: headlines, current affairs, light opinions.

Scene actions:

- Read a headline.
- Summarise gist.
- Ask "what do you think?"
- Agree/disagree politely.

### Railway Platform

Purpose: travel language with conversation repair.

Scene actions:

- Ask for information.
- Confirm details.
- Explain a problem.
- Ask someone to repeat.

### Cinema Or Cultural Event

Purpose: social invitations, reactions, narrative follow-up.

Scene actions:

- Invite.
- Accept/decline.
- Discuss afterwards.
- Explain what you liked.

## Phrase Families

Track phrase mastery by family:

- Opening and greeting.
- Asking follow-up questions.
- Giving opinions.
- Soft disagreement.
- Clarifying and repairing.
- Telling past events.
- Comparing.
- Explaining reasons.
- Summarising news.
- Making plans.

## Collaborative Practice

V1 should prepare for partner play but does not need full realtime multiplayer immediately.

Useful collaborative modes:

- Same-room pass-and-play: one device, two profiles.
- Parallel daily challenge: both users get related prompts and compare completion.
- Conversation relay: one user writes an Italian sentence, the other replies.
- Repair swap: each user fixes a sentence generated from the other's mistake pattern without exposing private notes unless opted in.
